# Pine Tree — Forest-First, Optional Personal Story Proposal

## Core conviction

The Pine Tree journey should first be encountered as **forest, season, gesture, and presence**. The user should not be asked to explain themselves before they have touched the soil, followed the root, and watched the tree mature. The Wood-element lesson is carried by the tree itself: grounding, resilience, yielding, branching, and widening are felt before they are personalized.

Personal meaning should therefore be an **invitation after experience**, not an assignment before it.

## Two complementary streams

| Stream | Purpose | Availability | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Forest Journey** | The original nine-day sensory experience: observe, touch, and reflect without recording anything. | Default for every new user. | Spacious, natural, non-demanding. |
| **Personal Story** | A private revisit that lets a user plant an intention and retain one short word from selected days. | Offered after first Day 9 completion; also accessible later from the side menu. | Intimate, optional, quietly authored. |

Both streams use the same tree, scenes, gestures, and nine-day progression. The difference is not a separate product; it is the degree of personal participation invited from the user.

## Forest Journey: the untouched first passage

The default journey would preserve the existing gold questions as contemplations only. There would be no text field, no saved word, no request to name an intention, and no personal data in the first passage. The user simply experiences the forest and its Wood-element rhythm.

The existing sound setting could still offer optional ambience when deliberately enabled, and the haptic language could still support physical presence. Neither turns the journey into journaling.

## Day 9: the invitation, not the extraction

After the forest opens and the existing **Mature.** card settles, the first completion would add a small, subdued text invitation beneath the primary return action:

> **Walk it again with an intention**

A one-sentence explanation would appear only when selected:

> *Carry a private word through the same forest. Nothing leaves this device.*

This does not ask the user to choose at the beginning or interrupt their final moment. It simply leaves a door open after they have received the full forest experience.

## Personal Story: an optional second relationship with the tree

When a user chooses the personal path, the side menu gains a quiet, two-item stream selector:

| Menu item | Supporting line |
|---|---|
| **Forest Journey** | Experience the tree as it is. |
| **Personal Story** | Carry a private intention through the forest. |

Within **Personal Story**, Day 1 offers a single optional field after the existing gold reflection:

> *Give this intention a few words (optional).* 

The user may leave it empty and still proceed. Later days do not ask for input while the practice is underway. Only after completion, a discreet text link can offer:

> **Keep a word from today**

The user may save a word, a phrase, or nothing at all. This preserves the tactile practice as the centre of each day.

## Personal Story return at Day 9

The Day 9 completion card changes only in Personal Story mode. Below the existing completion message, it may display:

> **From what you planted**  
> *“{Day 1 intention}”*

If the user saved later words, they appear as a simple, muted growth thread—not a dashboard, not a score, and not an analysis. If they saved nothing, the card remains identical to the Forest Journey completion.

## Sensory layer across both streams

The proposed optional sound and haptic system should sit beneath both streams. It supports the natural world rather than personal storytelling:

- Forest ambience remains off until deliberately enabled.
- Haptics confirm meaningful contact and completion, never every movement.
- Three ambient worlds—Root, Form, and Canopy—follow the tree’s growth regardless of stream.
- Personal Story adds no distinct “productivity” sounds, badges, or tracking signals.

## Why this is the premium choice

This structure treats privacy and slowness as part of the design. It gives the forest the authority to teach first, then gives the user an optional way to notice what the experience has opened in them. A user can return indefinitely to the pure world; they are never required to make their inner life into content.

## Recommended first implementation

1. Preserve the current experience as **Forest Journey**, with no fields anywhere in the initial passage.
2. Add the Day 9 secondary invitation, **Walk it again with an intention**.
3. Add the two-item side-menu selector and a local-only Personal Story mode.
4. In Personal Story only, add the Day 1 optional intention field, post-completion word capture, and Day 9 private return.
5. Keep the optional ambient sound and refined haptic language shared across both streams.
6. Store all Personal Story data locally and clear it through the existing reset action.

## Implementation validation

The default Forest Journey opening was visually confirmed to have no personal-intention field. A completed Day 9 Forest Journey state was then reached through the guided path, enabling the optional Personal Story invitation for visual validation.
The Day 9 Forest Journey completion presented the quiet `RETURN WITH AN INTENTION` invitation and the approved supporting line. Selecting it restarted at Day 1 in Personal Story mode, where the optional intention field appeared within the existing veil without changing the Forest Journey default.
A sample intention, `Steady roots`, was entered in Personal Story mode and Day 1 began normally with the field removed as the veil lifted. The original seed instruction and tactile target remained fully visible after the field was captured.
The Personal Story Day 9 completion retained the original forest completion message and added a quiet `FROM WHAT YOU PLANTED` return with the saved sample intention, `Steady roots`. The Forest Journey version did not show this return.
In Personal Story mode, the completed Day 2 card offered `KEEP A WORD FROM TODAY` after the tactile practice and after the normal continuation control. Forest Journey cards do not expose this optional capture action.
The Day 2 word-capture control opened a compact optional form within the completion card. A sample word, `Patience`, was entered for final persistence verification.
The sample Day 2 word, `Patience`, saved successfully in the local Personal Story record alongside the Day 1 intention. The Day 9 Personal Story completion was then re-opened for final visual confirmation of the growth thread.
Final validation confirmed that a fresh public session opens in Forest Journey mode with the original Day 1 veil unchanged and no personal field. The optional Personal Story path appears only after a completed Forest Journey Day 9 state, preserves the ritual UI, and returns saved intention and word entries only at Personal Story Day 9 completion.
