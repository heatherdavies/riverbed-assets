# Post-Begin Instruction Flow Proposal

## Current structure

For Days 2–9, the reflective opening card currently includes the practical instruction before the `BEGIN PRACTICE` button. The shared post-Begin ritual-copy block contains the same instruction field but is not presented as a focused, action-only second step.

## Proposed two-step flow

1. **Reflective opening:** Title, purpose line, gold reflection, quiet prompt, and `BEGIN PRACTICE` button only.
2. **After Begin Practice:** The opening card clears. A compact semi-transparent instruction panel appears below the day rail with the practical action in clear language, alongside the existing gesture cue and target. The panel is dismissible by a tap, but does not auto-disappear.

Day 1’s current veil-to-action sequence stays untouched. This change applies only to the reusable Days 2–9 flow.

## Validation

The fresh cache-busted build loaded and reached the Day 5 reflective opening state, ready for visual review before Begin Practice.
The Day 5 opening card was visually confirmed to contain only title, purpose, gold reflection, quiet prompt, and `BEGIN PRACTICE`. Pressing the button then revealed the compact `THE PRACTICE` instruction panel with the correct coil instruction and `TAP TO CONTINUE` cue.
The Day 5 instruction panel was tapped and dismissed successfully, leaving the coil and `SPIRAL UPWARD` cue unobstructed. A rapid automated loop across day transitions was not used as a validation result because image-ready transition gates defer some day changes; individual transitions must be checked sequentially.
Day 7 was loaded sequentially and its reflective opening remained concise and readable before Begin Practice, even with the longer gold reflection.
After Day 7 Begin Practice, the longer canopy instruction appeared in the post-Begin panel, wrapped cleanly, and retained the `TAP TO CONTINUE` dismissal cue alongside the existing `SWAY THE CANOPY` gesture hint.
Day 1 was checked separately: the existing veil-to-action opening remained visible and the new post-Begin instruction panel stayed hidden.

## Cue refinement validation

The cache-busted cue-revision build loaded successfully and reached the Day 5 opening state before checking the new immersive cue.
The Day 5 post-Begin panel was visually checked with the revised cue. `WHEN YOU ARE READY, BEGIN` remains legible beneath the practical instruction and preserves the panel’s calm ritual tone.
